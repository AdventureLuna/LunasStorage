# Storage system requirements

## Purpose
Keep track of everything I have at home, to find things, store things in a way that is findable, and create a way to audit items to throw away.

## What is being stored
A very high variance of things with different sizes, storage options and interchangability.

## Users
Just Luna.

## Organization and retrieval
Every item should have some kind of location and should be findable with some fuzzy semantic search to find it in many years when I forgot what it was called.

## Constraints
This is just for the digital system, all software used should be free.
